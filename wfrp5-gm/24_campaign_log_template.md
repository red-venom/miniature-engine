# Campaign Log Template

The GM fills this in when the player types `save` or ends a session. Fill every section; write "none" if empty.
Keep the Secrets section to facts the plot needs; the player agrees not to read it. The player saves it as
`campaign_log.md` in the project (replacing the old one) or pastes it at the start of the next chat.

```
# Campaign Log: <campaign name>

## Settings
- Dice mode: <own dice / GM rolls>
- Fortune offers: <ask / key / never>
- Standing defence: <Dodge / Melee (…)> · Fortune for Advantage on defence: <yes / no>
- Optional rules in use: <none, or e.g. [WoM] Revised Spellcasting> (supplement rules that change core rules; see 26_supplement_conversion.md)
- Sudden Death default for background NPCs: <yes / no> (confirmed at the start of each fight)
- Fumbles: <drop weapon (p. 165) / Oops! Table> (chosen once per campaign)

## Current situation
- Session number: <n>
- Session status: <in progress / ended>   (only "ended" restores Fortune at the next chat)
- End-of-session effects done: <e.g. Corruption Test for greatest exposure: Minor, passed>
- Fortune now: <n> of <max>
- In-game date and time (Imperial calendar, p. 289): <e.g. Backertag, 12 Sigmarzeit 2512, evening>
- Location: <place, region>
- Immediate situation: <one or two sentences: where the character is and what is happening>
- Active threats: <who or what is hunting or opposing the character>

## Adventure progress (published adventures, e.g. [EiS] Enemy in Shadows)
- Adventure: <none / Enemy in Shadows>   Solo mode: <companions / several PCs / scaled encounters>
- Current chapter and scene: <e.g. Ch 2 · Inn of the Seven Spokes>
- Flags set: <EiS.flag_name, EiS.flag_name, ...>
- Clues found (and by whom): <clue: where found>
- Key NPC states: <name: alive/dead, attitude, where>
- Adventure clock: <in-game date, e.g. 26 Jahrdrung 2512 (EiS Day 1); days left before scheduled events>

## Party
- <Player character name> (see character sheet)
- Companions, hirelings, animals: <name, role, key stats or bestiary entry, current Wounds, money, XP current/spent/total for each Henchman>

## Open threads and quests

## Important NPCs
| Name | Who they are | Attitude to party | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

## Places visited
- <place>: <what happened, anything left behind>

## GM rulings (not in the rules)

- <ruling and the situation it covers>

## XP awarded

|---|---|---|

## Session summaries

## Secrets (GM only — player agrees not to read)

- <hidden plot facts, NPC motives, upcoming events>
```
